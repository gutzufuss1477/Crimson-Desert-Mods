## Changes

- Dedicated tenth DLC tab for the existing 234 expansion offers, including Natural Pearl and 40 crafting/knowledge books. All remain at Alden.
- Long Horn and Water added to Materials in both versions, 1 Copper / 999 merchant stock. Both are base-game ingredients; native stack limits are preserved.
- Existing offer, purchase and stock/save identities retained. Original tab stocks, conditions and nine shared 1 Copper prices remain intact.
- Current DLC item IDs, 15-language names/descriptions, icon assets and book knowledge/action references checked.
- Original Better Inventory remains separate with no shared replacement paths; price composition with Equip All Armor and Steelheart passes in either order and on repeat application.

DMM's 4.7.3 changelog describes a fix for broken/missing DLC offers at ordinary vendors. Mount again with the updated manager and installed DLC. The earlier separate ASI crash report has not been reproduced and is not claimed individually fixed.

## Confirmation and verification

The author confirmed **DLC 17.4.3 working in game** on 7 November. The released ASI and game payloads are identical to the confirmed candidate; only the readme was finalized. No DLC 17.3.4 passed technical checks but was not separately tested in game; previous regular 17.3.3 was confirmed working.

Native/UI regressions, current-data checks and field-by-field price composition passed. Final staged packages and helpers passed a local Defender scan. Both published downloads were retrieved and verified byte-for-byte; Nexus page text, primary file, categories, DLC requirement and archived predecessors were read back through the API. No game files or DMM configuration were automatically changed.
