# Alden All-In-One Progression Shop v17

Released **2 November 2026**, for **Crimson Desert 2.04.05 / Steam build 26361232**.

[Complete package on Nexus](https://www.nexusmods.com/crimsondesert/mods/2066?tab=files&file_id=19088) · [Source and installation](../mods/alden-all-in-one-progression-shop/) · [Catalog/manifest recipe](../recipes/alden-v17-b26361232/) · [SHA-256 checksums](alden-v17-b26361232-SHA256SUMS.txt)

The catalog grows from 379 to **567 items at 1 Copper**, including rubber and additional crafting/progression supplies. Eight tabs group the inventory: All (567), Abyss (94), Abyss Plans (93), Books (71), Recipes (95), Blueprints (125), Materials (39) and Contracts (50). Other merchant records are preserved; no DLC-exclusive items are added.

Labels follow the game text language, with all 15 variants included. Tab widths follow the text and allow wrapping. Install the complete ZIP through **DMM 4.6.4** and enable both the data mod and its bundled **Alden Category Tabs.asi** helper. Disable older Alden versions, mount and restart. The helper and its matching data/UI target the stated game build.

Category filtering and purchases were author-confirmed in test 18c. English localization and wrapping were then author-confirmed in TEST 19 on 2 November. All 15 language tables passed data validation; the other languages were not separately visually tested. The final UI, catalog, stock, price and language payloads are byte-identical to TEST 19. The final native change is its diagnostic version text only; compilation, native callback tests and wrong-host refusal passed.
