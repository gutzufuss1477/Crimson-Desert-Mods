Mining, Bank, AllStats, Alden and Steelheart were confirmed working in-game on 31 October 2026. Speed-only passed 1,135 host checks plus native/loader checks; its separate gameplay test is pending. Native release payloads are unchanged from those checked builds.

**ASI installation:** close the game, remove older/test copies of the same mod, copy the ASI into `Crimson Desert/bin64`, keep your x64 ASI loader and existing INI settings. DMM is not required for these ASIs. Choose AllStats OR Speed-only, never both.

**Alden and Steelheart:** close the game, unmount, import the ZIP through DMM 4.5.1 or a compatible newer release, disable the previous version, mount and restart. Keep DMM's installed runtime and ASI loader. Alden retains the original 379-item catalog/stock/order; Steelheart changes only its equip-buff.

Assets include four ASI packages and the Steelheart manifest-only package. Alden's ready-to-install table overlay is on Nexus; its source and recipes are in Git. `SHA256SUMS.txt` covers all six Nexus packages.

Healthbar is excluded while its Multitarget fix is being tested. Bank Save Patcher is excluded. Older releases remain available for their labelled builds.
