ALDEN SHOP DLC 17.4.3
Crimson Desert / Steam build 26480954 / DMM 4.7.4 or later
Main version for players with installed Charting the Unknown.
Use the separate No DLC package when playing without the expansion.

NEW DLC TAB
All 234 existing DLC offers now have their own tenth category: DLC.
This includes 40 DLC crafting/knowledge books (doors, windows, decor and more),
18 Abyss gears, 18 Abyss plans, 19 recipes, 104 blueprints and 35 materials.
Natural Pearl remains included. All books stay at Alden.
The normal categories contain the base-game offers. All includes both sets.

Long Horn (Langhorn) and Water (Wasser) are added to Materials for 1 Copper,
with 999 merchant stock each. They are base-game ingredients. Normal stack
limits stay unchanged. The previous 841 offers retain their stock/save IDs.
The Original tab retains 40 native offers, conditions and stock limits;
its nine shared items retain the existing 1 Copper price.

843 offers: All 843, Abyss 94, Abyss Plans 93, Books 71, Recipes 95,
Blueprints 125, Materials 41, Contracts 50, Original 40, DLC 234.
Counters show catalog totals; sold-out or conditional Original offers may hide.

INSTALL / UPDATE
Use DMM 4.7.4 or later. DMM 4.7.3 fixed DLC items appearing as broken rows
at base-game vendors by adding the vendor to the DLC shop list. A fresh
mount with the updated manager and installed DLC is required.
Close the game and unmount. Completely replace the old Alden folder, including
the regular variant if active. Import Alden_Shop_DLC.zip, enable Alden Shop DLC
and Alden Shop DLC - 1 Copper. Replace/enable the included 17.4.3
Alden Category Tabs.asi through ASI management. Mount again and restart.
Keep only one Alden variant and one helper. Do not merge old folders or copy
the internal files directory into the game. Requires Microsoft Visual C++ v14 x64.

Original Better Inventory and Trade UI 3.3 remains optional and separate.
No shared UI replacement files or required load order. Follow its author's
DLC texture instructions. Original Equip All Armor remains separate.

VALIDATION
The catalog is checked against the current DLC files supplied on C:,
not only the older 2.04.05 reference. Native category/UI checks pass.
The author confirmed this DLC build working in game on 7 November 2026.
The released ASI and all game payloads are identical to that confirmed build;
only this readme was finalized afterward. The separate earlier ASI crash
report has not been reproduced. No DLC definitions, textures or models are
bundled: the installed expansion provides those resources.

CHECKS FOR PROBLEM REPORTS: confirm DMM detects the DLC and mounts without skipped mods; open Alden,
check the DLC tab, buy Natural Pearl and a door/window knowledge book, check
names/icons and learn the book. Buy Long Horn and Water from Materials.
Switch all tabs by mouse and keyboard/controller; close/reopen and check stock.
For a failure include mount_log.txt, Alden Category Tabs.asi.log and game build.

UNINSTALL: close the game, unmount, disable shop, price component and ASI,
then remount. Purchased items and money spent are not rolled back.
