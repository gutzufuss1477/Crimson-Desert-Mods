ALDEN SHOP - NO DLC 17.3.4
Crimson Desert / Steam build 26480954 / DMM 4.7.4 or later
Optional version for players without Charting the Unknown.

Adds Long Horn (Langhorn) and Water (Wasser) to Materials for 1 Copper,
with 999 merchant stock each. Both are base-game ingredients. Normal item
stack limits stay unchanged. All 607 previous offers keep their stock/save
identities, limits and prices. The Original tab still contains 40 offers;
its nine overlapping items keep the existing 1 Copper price.

609 total offers: All 609, Abyss 94, Abyss Plans 93, Books 71, Recipes 95,
Blueprints 125, Materials 41, Contracts 50, Original 40. No DLC tab or items.
Counts are catalog totals; original unlock and restock conditions still apply.

INSTALL / UPDATE
Close the game and unmount in DMM. Completely replace the old Alden folder;
do not merge folders. Import Alden_Shop_No_DLC.zip and enable its shop and
1 Copper component. Replace the old Alden Category Tabs.asi with the included
17.3.4 helper in DMM's ASI management. Keep only one Alden variant/helper
active. Mount again and restart. Do not copy internal files into the game.
Requires the Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable, x64.

Original Better Inventory and Trade UI 3.3 remains optional and separate.
There are no shared UI replacement files or required load order. Follow its
author's DLC/non-DLC texture instructions. Equip All Armor remains separate.

VALIDATION
Built against current base-game data. Native category/UI and wrong-process
checks pass. The author confirmed the companion DLC 17.4.3 in game on
7 November 2026; this No DLC variant has not had a separate gameplay test.
Previous regular 17.3.3 was confirmed working on the same day.
No game, save or DMM configuration is changed by the build process.

CHECKS FOR PROBLEM REPORTS: buy Long Horn and Water from Materials, switch categories using mouse,
keyboard/controller, close with ESC, reopen and check Original stock.
For problems include DMM's mount_log.txt and Alden Category Tabs.asi.log.

UNINSTALL: close the game, unmount, disable the shop, price component and ASI,
then remount. Purchased items and money spent are not rolled back.
