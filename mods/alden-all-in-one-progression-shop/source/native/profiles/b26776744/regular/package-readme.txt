Alden Shop No DLC 17.3.9 | Steam 26776744 | DMM 5.0.2

Close the game and unmount. Remove the previous Alden shop, any old 1 Copper component and its category helper in DMM. Import this ZIP once, enable Alden Shop No DLC and the included Alden Category Tabs under ASI Mods, mount and restart. The two editions are alternatives.

The base catalog has 606 offers including the 40 Original offers. Category tabs and catalog prices use the bundled helper. Of the 40 Original offers, 31 keep their regular prices. The nine overlapping items cost 1 Copper. Catalog item prices are shared with other shops for the rest of the session once Alden opens.
