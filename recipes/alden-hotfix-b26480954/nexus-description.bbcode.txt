[b]Game update hotfix - 6 November 2026[/b]
[b]Regular version 17.3.3[/b] and [b]optional DLC version 17.4.2[/b] support [b]Steam build 26480954[/b]. The previous category helpers support build 26448710 and refuse to activate on the new executable. [b]Replace both the shop package and its included Alden Category Tabs.asi.[/b]

This update refreshes the native helper and preserves the game's updated official language data. Assortments, prices, stock identities, nine category tabs and single-step keyboard/controller switching are unchanged. All items remain at Alden; no new merchant feature is included.

[b]Original assortment and progression[/b]
The Original tab keeps Alden's 40 original offers, native stocks and unlock/restock conditions. 31 retain normal prices; nine items shared with the mod catalog stay at 1 Copper in both places, with separate stock records. The regular catalog has 598 distinct items. Existing stock/save identities are retained; historical vanilla purchase counters are not migrated.
Tiered Abyss Gear deliberately stays at [b]Tier I[/b]. Tier II/III, including Vitality II/III, remain for crafting/synthesis.

[b]Requirements and update steps[/b]
Windows PC, Steam build 26480954, DMM with support for that game build and its ASI loader enabled, plus [url=https://aka.ms/vc14/vc_redist.x64.exe]Microsoft Visual C++ v14 Redistributable (x64)[/url]. Runtime DLLs are not bundled.
[list=1]
[*]Close the game and unmount in DMM.
[*]Completely replace the previous Alden package. Do not merge into an old folder.
[*]Import Alden_Shop.zip, or Alden_Shop_DLC.zip for DLC owners, and enable its matching 1 Copper component.
[*]Replace and enable the included Alden Category Tabs.asi in ASI Mods. The normal pair must be 17.3.3; the DLC pair must be 17.4.2.
[*]Mount, restart the game and visit Alden.
[/list]
Do not manually copy the internal files/ folder into the game. If the ASI is not imported automatically, extract it and add it through DMM's ASI management.

[b]Validation and DLC testing[/b]
Native category/UI regressions, current game-data checks and price composition passed for this hotfix. [b]These new builds have not yet been confirmed in game.[/b] Earlier normal-version gameplay confirmation applies to 17.3.2 on build 26448710.
[b]I do not own the DLC and cannot test it myself. DLC owners, please test the optional file and report results.[/b] Its catalog comes from DLC 2.04.05; no old DLC item tables, models or textures are bundled. A community member reported the earlier DLC shop working, but complete DLC validation remains outstanding. The separately reported ASI crash has not been reproduced; this executable-compatibility hotfix is not a confirmed fix for that report.
For reports, include the game build, DMM/loader versions, active ASI helpers, Alden Category Tabs.asi.log and the DMM mount log. Check purchases, tab switching, Original stock and DLC book learning.
The final ZIPs and ASIs passed local Microsoft Defender scans. The shared Microsoft runtime and Control Flow Guard are retained; the helpers are unsigned. Other engines and future signatures remain unverified.

[b]Uninstall[/b]
Close the game, unmount, disable the shop, its price component and category ASI, then remount. Purchased items and spent money are not rolled back.
